# User Interviews

---

## Interview 1: Aman Khan.

- **Role**: CTO
- **Company Stage**: Seed (12 employees)
- **Direct Quotes**:
  - "I have no idea who is on Cursor vs. who is just using Copilot. It's a mess of expense reports."
  - "The biggest fear isn't the $20/mo, it's the API bill that hits $4,000 because of one inefficient loop."
  - "I'd use this tool if it gave me a single PDF I could send to my CEO to justify a 'Standardize on Claude' policy."
- **Surprising Moment**: Alex was more worried about the _time_ spent managing licenses than the actual dollar amount of the licenses.
- **Design Change**: Added the "Primary Use Case" field to help justify why certain tools (like high-end API models) are necessary for specific teams.

---

## Interview 2: Sharath R.

- **Role**: Operations Manager
- **Company Stage**: Series A (45 employees)
- **Direct Quotes**:
  - "We're paying for 'Enterprise' on three different tools just for SSO. It feels like a tax."
  - "If you can show me how to save $1,000/mo, I'll book a call immediately."
  - "The UI needs to look like a 'Finance' tool, not a 'Hacker' tool, so our CFO takes it seriously."
- **Surprising Moment**: Sarah mentioned they have "zombie seats"—paid licenses for employees who left 3 months ago but were never offboarded from the AI tools.
- **Design Change**: Added a "Number of Seats" input per tool to calculate "Zombie Seat" waste.

## Interview 3: Nagendar.

- **Role**: Solo Founder
- **Company Stage**: Pre-seed / Bootstrapped
- **Direct Quotes**:
  - "I'm on every 'Pro' plan because I'm afraid of missing out on the best model, but I probably only use 10% of the features."
  - "I want to see how I compare to other solo founders. Am I overspending?"
- **Surprising Moment**: Jordan didn't realize that Gemini Advanced was included in some Google Workspace tiers they already paid for.
- **Design Change**: Added "Already-Optimal" logic to the audit engine to reward users who are already spending efficiently.
