# Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

## Target User

The exact target user for AI Spendly is the **VP of Engineering** or **Head of Operations** at a Series A to Series C startup (20–150 employees).

- **Pain Point**: They have dozens of engineers individually buying Cursor or Claude Pro licenses on company cards, leading to "SaaS sprawl."
- **Why now**: As the "AI Hype" matures into "AI Efficiency," CFOs are starting to ask for a breakdown of the $5k–$10k/mo "AI Tools" line item on the profit and loss (P&L) statement.

## Search & Scroll Intent

- **Google Search**: "Cursor business vs pro," "Claude team vs enterprise," "AI tool consolidation for startups," "How to save on OpenAI API."

## Distribution Channels

1.  **X (Twitter) Lists**: Directly reply to "build-in-public" founders and VCs who post about their tech stacks. Offer a "free audit" link in the replies.
2.  **Slack/Discord Communities**: Post the tool in high-density engineering groups like _LennysNewsletter Slack_, _Founder University_, or _WorkOS Community_.
3.  **Hacker News (Show HN)**: A technical audience that will appreciate the "hardcoded rules over AI math" approach.

## 30-Day $0 Budget Plan

- **Days 1-7**: Direct outreach to 50 "Series A" founders on X/LinkedIn with a personalized "I built this for you" message.
- **Days 8-14**: Launch on Product Hunt. The goal isn't just upvotes, but to collect 200+ audits to generate the first "State of AI Spend" report.
- **Days 15-21**: Create a "Viral Hook" thread on X: "We audited 200 startups and found they're overspending $1.2M on AI. Here are the 3 biggest mistakes."
- **Days 22-30**: Partner with one "Fractional CFO" for startups to offer this as part of their onboarding audit.

## The Unfair Advantage

Credex's unfair advantage is **Inventory Transparency**. While other audit tools just give advice, AI Spendly ends with a button: "Capture these savings now with Credex Credits." It bridges the gap from _insight_ to _transaction_ in one click.

## Week-1 Traction Metrics

- **100+ Completed Audits**.
- **15% Lead Capture Rate** (Email provided).
- **3+ High-Value Consultation Bookings** (Savings > $500/mo).
