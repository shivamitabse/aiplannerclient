# Economics

## Unit Economics of a Lead
For Credex, a "converted lead" is a company that purchases AI credits through the platform.

-   **Average Order Value (AOV)**: $12,000/year (based on a 20-seat team saving 30% on $40k total spend).
-   **Gross Margin on Credits**: 15% (Credex's take).
-   **LTV (Lifetime Value)**: $1,800/year gross profit per customer.
-   **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)**: Effectively $0 for the first 100 users via organic distribution. For scaled paid acquisition (LinkedIn ads), we target a $150 CAC per "Qualified Audit."

## Conversion Funnel
1.  **Cold Traffic**: 1,000 visitors.
2.  **Audit Completed**: 200 (20% conversion).
3.  **Lead Captured (Email)**: 40 (20% conversion from audit).
4.  **High-Savings Consultation Booked**: 8 (20% conversion from lead).
5.  **Closed Won (Credit Purchase)**: 2 (25% conversion from consultation).

**Yield**: 1,000 visitors → 2 customers = $24,000 ARR.

## Path to $1M ARR in 18 Months
To hit $1M ARR, Credex needs approximately **84 active customers** (assuming $12k ARR each).

**What must be true:**
1.  **Volume**: We need ~42,000 visitors total to generate the 84 customers based on the funnel above.
2.  **Retention**: AI credits are a recurring need. We must maintain a <10% annual churn rate.
3.  **Expansion**: As startups grow their team size, their "AI Spend" naturally scales, increasing our AOV without additional CAC.
4.  **Viral Coefficient**: Every shared public report must drive at least 1.2 new visitors back to the tool (k > 1).

## Math Breakdown
-   **Traffic needed per month**: 2,333 visitors.
-   **Audits per month**: 466.
-   **Monthly Leads**: 93.
-   **New Customers per month**: 4.6.
-   **18-Month Cumulative Customers**: ~83.
-   **Total ARR**: 83 * $12,000 = **$996,000**.

Approximate numbers show that this tool is a highly viable top-of-funnel engine for Credex.
