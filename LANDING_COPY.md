## FAQ

**1. Is my data secure?**
Absolutely. We don’t require a login or access to your billing accounts. You manually input your spend, and we calculate the savings locally. Identifying details are stripped from shared public reports.

**2. How do you calculate the savings?**
We use a deterministic audit engine powered by real-time pricing data from Cursor, Claude, OpenAI, and more. Our logic checks for tier-mismatches, tool redundancy, and API inefficiencies.

**3. Is this really free?**
Yes. AI Spendly is a free tool provided by Credex. We make money by helping high-spend teams switch to discounted AI infrastructure credits, but the audit itself is 100% free with no strings attached.

**4. Can I share my report with my CFO?**
Yes. Every audit generates a unique public URL (with sensitive info hidden) that you can share via Slack, email, or social media to justify stack consolidation.

**5. Which tools do you support?**
We support all major AI players: Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, Anthropic API, OpenAI API, Gemini, Windsurf, and v0. We update our pricing data weekly.
